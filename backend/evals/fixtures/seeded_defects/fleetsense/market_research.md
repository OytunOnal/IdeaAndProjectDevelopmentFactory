# FleetSense — Market Research

## Market sizing
- **TAM:** last-mile/local delivery software for small commercial fleets, EU+TR+UK: fleets of 5-50 vans estimated at ~400k companies (~4.5M vans); routing/telematics spend potential ~$1.8B/yr (confidence: medium).
- **SAM:** fleets planning routes manually today (no telematics contract): survey-based estimates put this at 50-65% of the 5-50 van segment → ~220k fleets.
- **SOM (36 months):** one metro-region-at-a-time GTM through associations and leasing partners: ~2,500 fleets reachable; at $120/fleet/mo → ~$3.6M ARR obtainable ceiling.

## Trends
- E-commerce keeps pushing delivery volume to local couriers; fuel and driver costs rose faster than delivery prices — efficiency tooling demand is real and cost-justified.
- Enterprise telematics (Samsara, Webfleet) moves down-market slowly; their per-vehicle hardware pricing stays awkward for 10-van fleets.
- Headwind: gig-logistics platforms absorb some small-courier volume and dictate their own routing.

## Target customer validation
- Primary: dispatchers at local courier/delivery firms — planning in spreadsheets/WhatsApp, 60-90 min/day on route puzzles; highly reachable via regional associations.
- Pains validated in industry surveys: no live picture of the day, customer "where is my delivery" calls, fuel waste on unoptimized routes.
- Willingness-to-pay: fleets already pay per-van for insurance telematics boxes; a software-only $8/van/mo sits under the psychological threshold IF savings are demonstrable on their own data.

## Timing
OR-Tools-class solvers and commodity GPS make enterprise-grade optimization buildable by a 2-person team; the down-market gap is open now but Circuit/Routific-class players are adjacent — a regional, association-led wedge is the window.

## Verdict
Real pain, provable ROI, reachable niche; the risk is channel speed, not demand.

VIABILITY: strong
