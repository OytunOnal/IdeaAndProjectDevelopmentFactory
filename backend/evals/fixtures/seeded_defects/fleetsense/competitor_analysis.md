# FleetSense — Competitor Analysis

## Direct competitors
| Product | What it does | Pricing | Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Circuit for Teams | Route optimization for small fleets | ~$100-200/mo per depot | Polished driver app | Per-depot pricing stings multi-van shops; thin live ops view |
| Routific | Route planning SaaS | ~$49/vehicle/mo | Strong solver UX | Price point 6× our target; NA-centric |
| OptimoRoute | Routing + tracking | ~$35/driver/mo | Feature depth | Per-driver pricing; setup complexity |
| Samsara / Webfleet | Enterprise telematics | hardware + $30-60/vehicle/mo | Live everything | Contracts+hardware; overkill below 50 vans |
| Google Maps (manual) | The real incumbent | free | Familiar | No multi-stop optimization at fleet scale; no ops layer |

## Indirect competitors
- Spreadsheet + WhatsApp dispatching (dominant in the 5-50 segment)
- Leasing/insurer-bundled telematics boxes (tracking without routing)

## Gap analysis
- Nobody serves the segment at a **flat, low per-van software-only price** with dispatcher-first UX; competitors price per driver/depot or demand hardware.
- Live day view + proof-of-delivery in ONE simple tool is rare down-market; association/leasing distribution channels are unused by the incumbents.

## Positioning recommendation
- Price: $8/van/mo flat — visibly under Routific/OptimoRoute math for a 15-van fleet.
- Feature: "your dispatcher's morning, 10 minutes not 60" — outcome-first.
- Audience: dispatchers as champions; owners see the cost report.

## Differentiation strategy
1. Software-only, per-van flat pricing that a 12-van fleet can approve without a meeting.
2. Association- and leasing-channel distribution with try-on-your-own-stops demos.
3. Driver-app pragmatism (big targets, offline tolerance, cheap Androids) as a first-class design constraint.
