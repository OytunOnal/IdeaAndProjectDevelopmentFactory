# FleetSense — Go-to-Market Strategy

## Positioning
"Enterprise routing brains at a corner-shop price." TomTom/Verizon-class optimization without per-seat pricing or a sales call.

## Channels (priority order)
1. **Van leasing & fleet insurance partnerships** — leasing firms touch every small fleet at contract time; bundle a 3-month FleetSense trial into lease packages, rev-share on conversions
2. **Local courier associations** — Turkey & EU small-logistics associations run member newsletters and annual meets; sponsor the practical workshops, not the gala
3. **Direct outbound** — scraped-with-consent public courier registries; short demo video + "send us one day's stop list, we'll show your savings" offer
4. **Word of fleet** — this market talks in WhatsApp groups; referral = 2 months free per referred fleet

## Pricing motion
Self-serve at **$8/van/month** (aligned with the PRD); 14-day trial, no card upfront. The demo-with-your-own-stops offer is the primary conversion weapon — savings are visible in one screen.

## Launch phases
- Month 1-3: 10 design-partner fleets (free), weekly ride-alongs with dispatchers
- Month 4: paid launch in one metro region; case study per design partner
- Month 5-12: expand region by region following association calendars

## Targets
- 120 paying fleets (~1,800 vans) by month 12 → ~$14.4k MRR
- Trial→paid conversion ≥ 25% (the with-your-own-data demo should carry this)

## Risks
Long fleet decision cycles (owner + dispatcher must both say yes) → mitigate by making the dispatcher the champion with a tool she loves by day 3.
