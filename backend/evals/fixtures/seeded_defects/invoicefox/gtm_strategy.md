# InvoiceFox — Go-to-Market Strategy

## Positioning
"The polite collections agent for freelancers." Not accounting software — a focused tool for one painful job: getting paid.

## Channels (priority order)
1. **Freelancer communities** — genuinely useful content (late-payment email scripts, rate guides) in r/freelance, Indie Hackers, design/dev Slacks; no drive-by promotion
2. **SEO content** — "how to chase an unpaid invoice" cluster; high-intent, low-competition queries
3. **Accountant & bookkeeper referrals** — they see the unpaid-invoice pain first-hand; simple rev-share partner program
4. **Template marketplace** — free, shareable reminder-email template pack as top-of-funnel

## Pricing motion
Free plan is the growth engine (every reminder email footer: "sent via InvoiceFox"); Pro upgrade triggered at the 5-invoice limit or when analytics unlock matters.

## Launch phases
- Month 1-2: closed list of 50 freelancers from communities, weekly feedback loop
- Month 3: public launch (Product Hunt + community posts)
- Month 4-12: double down on the channel with the best CAC, kill the rest

## Targets
- 5,000 registered / 600 paying users by month 12
- Blended CAC under $40 by month 6

## Risks
Email-deliverability reputation is existential; a single spam-listing event could stall growth — mitigations live in the architecture plan.
