# PrepNest — Competitor Analysis

## Direct & adjacent competitors
- **GoStudent / Preply** — full-hour tutor marketplaces at $25-60/hour. Strong supply and brand, but the unit of sale (the hour) is exactly what target families can't afford regularly. Neither offers a sub-30-minute format.
- **Photomath / Gauth** — camera-solver apps, free or ~$10/month. Instant but answer-shaped: they solve the problem without unblocking the student, and exam parents distrust them as "cheating apps." No human, no accountability.
- **Khan Academy** — free, excellent content, no live human help and no alignment with the national exam syllabus's ordering and question style.
- **Local prep-course chains** — $800-2,500/year classroom packages; the incumbent budget line PrepNest partially displaces. Group format, fixed schedule, zero personalization on stuck concepts.

## Gap analysis
Nobody sells the **15-minute human unblock**: cheaper than an hour, more trustworthy than a solver, live unlike content libraries. The micro-format also unlocks a tutor supply pool (university students) that can't commit to hour-blocks but can absorb evening 15-minute slots.

## Defensibility notes
- The format is copyable by the hour-marketplaces; the moat must come from **exam-syllabus-specific matching + safeguarding trust with parents**, both slow to retrofit
- Solver apps could bolt on human sessions, but their brand is toxic to the parent-payer; parent trust is the contested resource
- Prep-course chains are potential channel partners rather than pure competitors (white-label micro-help for their enrolled students)

## Pricing landscape
$19/month with 3 human sessions undercuts a single tutoring hour while sitting above content-app pricing — the "real help, sane price" slot is currently empty.
