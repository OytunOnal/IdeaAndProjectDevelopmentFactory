# PrepNest — Product Requirements Document

## Problem
High-schoolers preparing for national university entrance exams get stuck on single concepts, and full-hour private tutoring at $40-80/hr is unaffordable for most families. What they need is ten focused minutes on the exact thing blocking them, tonight.

## Target users
- Primary: high-school students aged 15-18 in the final two exam-prep years
- Secondary: parents (the payer; want progress visibility, not lesson content)

## Core features (v1)
1. Stuck-concept booking — student snaps the problem, picks a time slot within the next 2 hours
2. Booked micro-sessions — 15-minute 1-on-1 video help on a single stuck concept; Plus plan includes 3 sessions per month
3. Tutor matching — vetted university-student tutors, matched by subject and exam track
4. Question bank + progress tracker — per-topic mastery view shared with the parent

## Pricing
- Free: 1 micro-session/month, question bank with daily cap
- Plus: $19/month — 3 sessions included, unlimited question bank, parent progress digest; extra sessions $5 each

## Scope
- In (v1): micro-session booking, video sessions, tutor vetting, question bank, parent digest
- Out (v1): group study rooms, full-hour lessons, homework-scanning solver, native tablet apps, self-serve tutor marketplace

## Safeguarding & minors' privacy
Users are minors. v1 ships with: verified parental consent at signup, background-checked tutors only, all sessions recorded and retained 90 days for review, in-session reporting button, no direct-message channel outside booked sessions, and GDPR-K/COPPA-aligned data handling.

## Success metrics
- 55% of Plus subscribers book all 3 included sessions in their first month
- <6% monthly churn on Plus after month 3
