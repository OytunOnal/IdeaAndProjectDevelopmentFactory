# PrepNest — Go-to-Market Strategy

## Positioning
"Fifteen minutes on the exact thing you're stuck on — for the price of two coffees a month, not $60 an hour." We are not a tutoring marketplace and not a solver app; we sell unblocking.

## Launch phases
- **Month 1:** closed launch with a 100-student waitlist recruited from two exam-prep subreddit/Discord communities; tutor supply pre-seeded from two partner universities' education faculties
- **Month 2:** school outreach begins — study-hall intro sessions via teacher contacts; public app-store launch
- **Months 3-6:** exam-season push (the category's demand triples in the final 4 months); creator campaign scales with what converted in month 2

## Acquisition channels
1. **Study-tube / study-gram creators** — mid-tier exam-prep creators (50-300k followers); pay-per-install trials in month 2, revenue share for the top 3 performers after
2. **Exam-prep communities** — genuinely useful weekly "hardest question, solved in 15 minutes" clips cut from consented, anonymized session recaps
3. **School ambassadors** — teachers get a free classroom dashboard in exchange for intro sessions at study halls.
4. **Parent word-of-mouth** — the weekly digest is shareable by design; parents recruiting parents is the cheapest trusted channel in this category

## Targets
- 6,000 registered / 700 paying subscribers by month 12
- Blended CAC under $25 by month 6
- Tutor supply: 150 active tutors by exam season, utilization ≥60% in peak weeks

## Seasonality note
This is a seasonal category: churn spikes the month after the exam. The counter-move is the mastery map rolling over to the next academic year and a "little sibling" referral offer at season end.
